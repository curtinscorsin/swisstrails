# Photograph review record

## 2026-08-17 catalogue pass

All 304 published photographs were rendered in labelled contact sheets and
visually inspected alongside their published destination names. The automated
catalogue audit separately checks that every image has a unique URL, a Commons
file page, a named creator, a reusable licence and sufficient resolution.

The visual pass found and corrected these unsuitable selections:

- **Pilatus:** replaced a Pilatus P3 aircraft with `Pilatus Autumn Sunset`.
- **Val Müstair:** replaced a pear-bread product photograph with a view of
  Santa Maria Val Müstair.
- **Oberblegisee:** replaced unrelated photographs from Balgach and Cartigny
  with two photographs explicitly identified as Oberblegisee.
- **Monte San Giorgio:** replaced two repetitive Lake Lugano views with a
  summit view and an exact-location geological-strata photograph.

Every replacement retains its Wikimedia Commons file page, creator and licence
in `apps/app/data/gallery-images.generated.json`.

This is an AI-assisted visual and metadata review, not a claim of human or
on-site verification. Before a commercial launch, the product owner or a named
human editor should still sign off the complete gallery and record any disputed
selection as under review.
